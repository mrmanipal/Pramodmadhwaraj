Journey

Udupi to the national stage

Born in Udupi in 1968, he came up through student and youth politics before his first election as MLA.

  1. 1968

    Born in Udupi, into a family already part of the district's public life.

  2. Early years

    Youth leadership in the NSUI and the Youth Congress.

  3. 2013

    Elected MLA for Udupi, as a first-time legislator.

  4. 2015

    Appointed Parliamentary Secretary (Revenue).

  5. 2016

    Became Minister of State, then Cabinet Minister for Fisheries, Youth Empowerment and Sports under Chief Minister Siddaramaiah. Also Udupi District In-charge Minister. The booklet names Oscar Fernandes as his guide.

  6. March 2018

    Ranked No. 1 among 224 MLAs in a Times of India / Prajavani survey.

  7. 12 May 2018

    Contested Udupi in the Assembly election as the Congress candidate. The 2018 booklet was published for this campaign.

  8. 7 May 2022

    Joined the BJP.

  9. 2023

    Campaigned across 13 constituencies in the Karnataka Assembly election.

  10. 21 Nov 2026

    Appointed National General Secretary of the BJP OBC Morcha, to organise OBC Morcha units and represent backward and fishing communities.

The coast

Fishermen first

His father built Malpe harbour. As Fisheries Minister he raised subsidies and relief for boat owners and fisherwomen. Switch between before and after.

Bridges that replaced boat crossings

Cost in ₹ crore. The Malpe–Padukere bridge is 315 m long and ended about 60 years of dependence on boats.

Roads

3,348 road works sanctioned

The largest single sector, at ₹468 crore, across PWD, CRF, Namma Grama Namma Raste, PMGSY, Nagarotthana-3, coastal authority and SC/ST colony roads.

Youth and sports

Ajjarkad stadium, upgraded

Part of ₹25.99 crore for youth and sports. Udupi hosted a national women's sports meet, state rural sports three times, a state youth mela three times and a state youth fest.

    Family

    A public-service household

    Malpe Madhwaraj

    His father. MLA for Udupi in 1962, builder of Malpe harbour and the town's rise as a fisheries hub. The M Madhwaraj Charitable Trust carries his name.

    Manorama Madhwaraj

    His mother. MLA, minister and Member of Parliament, known for 5-cent and 3-cent housing sites for the poor.
